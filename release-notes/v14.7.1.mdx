_November 24th, 2026_

:::caution
This release fixes [CVE-2026-111497](https://www.tenable.com/cve/CVE-2026-111497), a critical out-of-bounds write in the Brotli decoder of the Node.js runtime, upgrading all Console components to patched Node.js versions.
:::

## How to update your Console

For self-hosted installations, please head to the [self hosted upgrade guide](/docs/infrastructure/self-hosted/installation-chart/how-to-upgrade) or contact your Mia-Platform referent and upgrade to _Console Helm Chart_ `v15.0.13-9`.
