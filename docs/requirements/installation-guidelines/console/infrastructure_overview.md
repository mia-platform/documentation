Mia-Platform Console can be deployed in three different models, depending on your infrastructure requirements and operational preferences:

- **Platform as a Service (PaaS)**: Console is operated as a fully managed environment by Mia Platform — no infrastructure to provision or maintain. Your development teams get a ready-to-use Console instance from day one. Further information is available in the [PaaS overview](/requirements/installation-guidelines/console/paas/overview.md).
- **Bring Your Own Infrastructure (BYOI)**: Console is delivered as a managed service by Mia Platform, but the runtime infrastructure stays under your control. Further information is available in the [BYOI overview](/requirements/installation-guidelines/console/byoi/overview.md).
- **Self Hosted**: Purchase the Mia-Platform license and run Console entirely on your own infrastructure — on-premises or your cloud. You own the full stack: installation, upgrades, and operations. Further information is available in the [Self-Hosted requirements](/requirements/installation-guidelines/console/self-hosted/self-hosted-requirements.md).
